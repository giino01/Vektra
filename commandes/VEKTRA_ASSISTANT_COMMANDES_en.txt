================================================================================
         VEKTRA ASSISTANT — FULL LIST OF CAPABILITIES & COMMANDS
================================================================================

Vektra Assistant is your J.A.R.V.I.S.-style on-board voice copilot, designed
specifically for your BYD vehicle (DiLink 3.0 / 4.0) and Vektra 2.0.
It has full access to every piece of hardware in the car, to real-time
telemetry, to ADB system commands, to ambient lighting synchronisation and to
Groq artificial intelligence (Llama 3.3 70B).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WAKE WORD, CALLING IT & MEDIA VOLUME CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• How to call it:
  - Simply say: "Vektra" / "Hey Vektra" / "OK Vektra"
  - Or state your request directly: "Vektra, open the sunroof"
  - Or tap the voice status card in the Assistant tab or the 3D avatar.

• Stop everything in one gesture:
  - A single tap on the on-screen voice animation immediately cuts off the
    current reply, cancels the request and closes the animation. Handy when
    Vektra was triggered by mistake or won't stop.

• Automatic ducking of the normal speaker (Media):
  - As soon as you call Vektra, the normal speaker volume (radio, Spotify,
    Bluetooth music — the STREAM_MUSIC stream) automatically drops to 20% while
    you speak and while Vektra answers.
  - The driver headrest speaker volume (Navi 14 stream) stays at 100% for
    optimal voice clarity.
  - As soon as Vektra finishes answering, media volume is restored smoothly.

• Standby and voice wake-up:
  - "Vektra, go to sleep" / "Go on standby"
  - "Vektra, wake up"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. LANGUAGE PERSONALISATION, LANGUAGE MIRRORING & VARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Smart language mirroring (Dynamic Mirroring):
  - Vektra detects and adapts in real time to the way you express yourself:
    * Relaxed / casual / humorous tone: it replies in a warm, witty, lively and
      complicit way, matching your energy.
    * Serious / formal / technical tone: it adopts the stance of a chief
      automotive engineer and elite copilot — surgical and concise.

• Choice of French variant (Belgium or France):
  - Configurable in the assistant settings (French variant):
    * Belgium: natural, authentic use of the numbers "septante" (70) and
      "nonante" (90), and Belgian idioms and turns of phrase.
    * France: standard metropolitan French (soixante-dix, quatre-vingt-dix).

• Full multilingual support (6 voice languages):
  - System voice and replies available in French, English, Italian, German,
    Portuguese and Turkish, with no foreign phonetics mixed in.

4. LIVE INTERNET SEARCH & UNFILTERED INTELLIGENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Unlimited real-time web search (via DuckDuckGo):
  - Vektra consults the internet live and phrases its answer with no cut-offs or
    pointless intermediate messages.
  - Examples:
    * "Vektra, what's the weather forecast for tomorrow in Brussels?"
    * "Vektra, what was the score of Real Madrid's last match?"
    * "Vektra, what's today's economic news?"

• Freedom of expression & long-term memory (Grok style):
  - Intelligence with no prudish filter or moralising censorship, able to
    discuss any subject with authenticity, candour and complicity.
  - Remembers names, habits, favourite destinations and preferences.

14. OFFLINE OPERATION & STANDBY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Essential commands without a network:
  - When the car has no connection, the hardware commands keep working: windows,
    roof, sunshade, trunk, doors, air conditioning, seats, lighting, drive modes,
    navigation and music.
  - It's free conversation, web search and AI-written replies that become
    unavailable.
  - Can be enabled in the assistant settings ("Offline commands").

• Standby and wake-up:
  - "Vektra, go to sleep" / "Vektra, go on standby"
  - "Vektra, wake up"
  - These two phrasings exist in all ten languages of the app. No other phrase
    triggers standby: saying "close the window" does not put it to sleep.

• Deep sleep, car switched off:
  - When the ignition is cut, the assistant stops listening completely so as not
    to drain the battery, and starts up again when the ignition is turned on.

16. INTELLIGENCE, MEMORY & ADVANCED COMMANDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Chain several commands in one sentence:
  - "Vektra, close the windows, set the A/C to 21 and turn on my seat"
  - Vektra does everything in order and confirms in a single sentence.

• Correct yourself or undo right away:
  - "Vektra, open the rear right window… no, the rear left"
  - "Vektra, undo" (reverses the last action)

• Delayed actions:
  - "Vektra, in 10 minutes, close the windows"
  - "Vektra, when I arrive, close the roof"

• Reminders:
  - "Vektra, remind me to take the parcel when I arrive"
  - "Vektra, remind me to call the garage at the next start-up"

• Rules dictated by voice:
  - "Vektra, if it rains, close the windows"
  - "Vektra, if it's cold, turn on my seat"
  - Recognised conditions: rain, cold, hot, low battery, while driving, when parked, night.

• Clear everything:
  - "Vektra, forget your rules" / "Forget what's pending" / "Forget my habits"

• Habit memory:
  - Vektra remembers what you often ask at the same time and offers it to you.
  - Can be enabled in the assistant settings.

• Find a charging point:
  - "Vektra, find me a charging point"
  - Vektra searches, announces the point and starts guidance in your favourite navigation app.

• Questions about the car:
  - "Vektra, what's that warning light on the dashboard?"
  - "Vektra, which model am I driving?" / "Is my car electric or hybrid?"
  - "Vektra, how do I set the regeneration?"

• Explain-me mode (one step at a time):
  - "Vektra, explain how to do …"

• Spoken translation:
  - "Vektra, how do you say 'where is the charging point' in Dutch?"

• Calculations, percentages and conversions (no network needed):
  - "Vektra, 45 euros divided by three plus a 10% tip?"

• Summarised web search:
  - Vektra answers in two sentences; say "cite your source" to get the source.

• Eco-driving coach (on request):
  - "Vektra, how am I driving?"

• Assistant character (setting):
  - Minimal, Balanced or Warm. Only changes how it answers, never what it can do.

• Follow the spoken language (setting):
  - Answers a passenger in their language, then returns to yours.
