================================================================
VEKTRA — Co-pilot features
================================================================

• Speed camera & traffic alerts
  Fixed, mobile, average-speed and red-light cameras, roadworks and slowdowns. Announced about 15 seconds ahead (300, 500 or 1000 m depending on speed), reminder closer, beep when passing the camera.

• Speed camera database
  Updated automatically every week.

• Alert types
  Tick the alerts you want to hear.

• Voice Parking Guidance
  Voice announcement of parking distances in maneuvers and reverse · 100% voice

• Battery & Energy Monitoring
  Preventive alerts under 20% and cold weather charging advice

• Low 12 V battery alert
  Vektra warns when the 12 V battery voltage stays below 11.8 V (if the car provides it).

• Tires & Road Safety
  Abnormal pressure drop and black ice risk under 3°C

• Driver Fatigue Alert
  Friendly break suggestion after 2 hours of continuous driving

• Rain → window reminder
  Vektra reminds you to close the windows (just an alert, no question asked).

• Fog alert
  Warns when the weather reports fog at your position while you are driving. Once per trip.

• Offline announcement
  Says when the network drops and what still works, then announces its return.
