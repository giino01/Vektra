## Co-pilot — Your co-pilot, always alert

Each alert can be turned on or off in the app's Co-pilot tab.

| Feature | |
| --- | --- |
| **Speed camera & traffic alerts** | Fixed, mobile, average-speed and red-light cameras, roadworks and slowdowns. Announced about 15 seconds ahead (300, 500 or 1000 m depending on speed), reminder closer, beep when passing the camera. |
| **Speed camera database** | Updated automatically every week. |
| **Alert types** | Tick the alerts you want to hear. |
| **Voice Parking Guidance** | Voice announcement of parking distances in maneuvers and reverse · 100% voice |
| **Battery & Energy Monitoring** | Preventive alerts under 20% and cold weather charging advice |
| **Low 12 V battery alert** | Vektra warns when the 12 V battery voltage stays below 11.8 V (if the car provides it). |
| **Tires & Road Safety** | Abnormal pressure drop and black ice risk under 3°C |
| **Driver Fatigue Alert** | Friendly break suggestion after 2 hours of continuous driving |
| **Rain → window reminder** | Vektra reminds you to close the windows (just an alert, no question asked). |
| **Fog alert** | Warns when the weather reports fog at your position while you are driving. Once per trip. |
| **Offline announcement** | Says when the network drops and what still works, then announces its return. |

## Installation — Ready in 7 steps

| # | Installation |
| :---: | --- |
| **1** | **Download the APK** — Your choice: with the tablet's browser, on a USB stick, or via Telegram on the tablet. |
| **2** | **Install VEKTRA** — Open the downloaded file and confirm the installation. |
| **3** | **Grant the app's permissions** — Accept every permission requested on first launch. |
| **4** | **Grant the ADB permissions** — Accept the ADB authorisation request shown by the tablet. |
| **5** | ⏱ **Wait 30 seconds** — After opening VEKTRA for the first time, leave the tablet alone for about 30 seconds. |
| **6** | **Restart the tablet** — One restart, and everything is in place. |
| **7** | ✅ **Choose and start** — In the Cluster tab: your model, your display mode, then start the projection. Updates then happen directly in the app. |
